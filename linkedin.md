## 🚀 Option 1: The High-Impact Launch & Storytelling Post (Recommended)

🌾 **Excited to share what I’ve been building: KrishiMitra (कृषि मित्र) — An AI-Powered Digital Agriculture Platform built for Indian Farmers!** 🚜🇮🇳

Over 140 million farmers in India face critical decisions every season:
* Which crop gives the highest yield for their soil's specific N-P-K & pH levels?
* When should they spray fertilizers or irrigate based on real-time weather forecasts?
* What are the live APMC Mandi rates to ensure fair pricing for their harvest?

To solve this, I built **KrishiMitra** — a full-stack, production-ready agro-tech platform that transforms complex agronomic research into simple, everyday actionable decisions for farmers.

---

### 🌟 Key Features & Innovations:

1. 🧠 **AI Crop Recommendation**: Supervised Machine Learning model (Random Forest, 98%+ match accuracy) validating N, P, K, pH, rainfall, and seasonal compatibility grounded in ICAR guidelines.
2. 🌦️ **Hyperlocal Agromet Weather Advisory**: 5-day forecasts with crop stage-specific advisories (irrigation timing, wind thresholds for spraying, disease precautions).
3. 📊 **APMC Mandi Price Tracker**: Live commodity mandi rates (Min, Max, Modal prices per quintal) across Indian districts.
4. 🧪 **Soil Health Card Diagnostics**: Automated nutrient grading and balanced fertilizer/manure recommendations.
5. 🤖 **Multilingual Krishi AI Assistant**: Real-time voice/text conversational assistant supporting **English and हिन्दी (Hindi)**.
6. 🌿 **Immersive 3D WebGL Nature Experience**: Procedural living woodland backdrop powered by Three.js with dynamic lighting and floating spores for a serene, organic user experience.
7. 📧 **Automated Welcome & Onboarding**: Automated email delivery dispatching personalized welcome guides on farmer registration.

---

### 💻 The Tech Stack:

* **Frontend**: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Three.js (WebGL), Lucide Icons, i18next (Bilingual EN/HI).
* **Backend**: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Pydantic v2, Background Tasks.
* **Machine Learning**: Scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, Joblib.
* **Deployment & CI/CD**: 
  - Frontend: **Vercel** ⚡
  - Backend: **Render** (Containerized FastAPI service) 🚀

## 🏆 Option 3: Hackathon / Mission-Driven Story Post

Agriculture is the backbone of India, yet smallholder farmers often struggle with fragmented information, unpredictable weather, and volatile market prices.

I wanted to bridge this gap between agronomic science and on-ground farming. That's why I created **KrishiMitra (कृषि मित्र)**.

From testing soil nutrient cards to predicting the best crop variety using Machine Learning and tracking live Mandi prices, KrishiMitra puts actionable intelligence right into the farmer's hands — in their own language.

Building this project taught me so much about:
- Designing farmer-friendly, high-contrast, accessible UI.
- Integrating procedural 3D WebGL scenes using **Three.js** that run smoothly on mobile & desktop.
- Deploying resilient, decoupled micro-architectures with **FastAPI** on Render and **React** on Vercel.
