# KrishiMitra Agricultural Knowledge Base

## Major Crops in India

### 1. Rice (Paddy)
- **Soil Type**: Clayey or clay loams with good water holding capacity.
- **pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.5
- **Temperature**: 20°C - 35°C
- **Rainfall**: 150 - 300 cm
- **Key Nutrients**: High Nitrogen (N), Moderate Phosphorus (P) and Potassium (K).
- **Major Pests**: Stem borer, Brown planthopper.
- **Common Diseases**: Blast, Bacterial leaf blight.

### 2. Wheat
- **Soil Type**: Well-drained loams and clay loams.
- **pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Temperature**: 15°C - 25°C
- **Rainfall**: 75 - 100 cm
- **Key Nutrients**: Balanced N-P-K (4:2:1 ratio).
- **Major Pests**: Aphids, Armyworm.
- **Common Diseases**: Rust (Brown, Yellow, Black), Karnal bunt.

### 3. Maize (Corn)
- **Soil Type**: Deep, fertile, well-drained sandy loam to silty loam.
- **pH Range**: 5.8 - 7.5
- **Temperature**: 21°C - 27°C
- **Rainfall**: 60 - 110 cm
- **Key Nutrients**: High N and P demand during early vegetative stages.
